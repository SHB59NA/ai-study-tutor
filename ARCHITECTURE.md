## Design Principles

1. **Retrieval before generation** — generated content is conditioned on evidence retrieved from the uploaded document.
2. **Source grounding** — prompts explicitly prohibit outside facts for document-based tutoring.
3. **Visible evidence** — answers expose PDF page references and retrieved passages.
4. **Graceful uncertainty** — unsupported questions should be refused rather than guessed.
5. **Learner adaptation** — explanations support beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels.
6. **Transparent progress model** — mastery is based on a small recent score history rather than an opaque predictive model.
7. **Concept-level support** — quiz questions include concept labels so weak areas can be detected from performance.
8. **Targeted review loop** — the tutor can automatically retrieve and explain the weakest concept from the source material.
9. **Educator-support framing** — the system is designed as learning support, not as a replacement for educators.

## Current Limitations

- learner progress is in memory and resets when the application restarts or a new document is uploaded
- concept labels are generated by the LLM and have not yet been normalized across semantically similar labels
- the mastery thresholds are prototype heuristics, not validated educational assessment thresholds
- retrieval currently uses TF-IDF rather than semantic embeddings
